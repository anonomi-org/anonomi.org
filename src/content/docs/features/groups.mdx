Anonomi Messenger supports two types of group communication: **private groups** for trusted circles and **open groups (forums)** for broader communities.

---

## Private groups

Private groups are invite-only spaces where all messages are end-to-end encrypted and synced peer-to-peer.

- **Invite-only** — only people you invite can join.
- **Encrypted** — all messages, media, and metadata are encrypted end-to-end.
- **Peer-to-peer sync** — messages sync directly between group members without passing through a central server.

### Creating a private group

1. Open the main navigation.
2. Tap **Create Group**.
3. Add contacts to the group.
4. Give the group a name.
5. Tap **Create**.

### Managing members

The group creator can:
- Add new members by sending invitations.
- Remove members from the group.

---

## Open groups (forums)

Open groups are public discussion spaces that anyone with an invitation link can join. They support threaded conversations for organized discussion.

- **Invitation link** — share a link to let anyone join.
- **Threaded replies** — reply to specific messages to keep discussions organized.
- **Moderation** — group creators can manage members and content.

### Creating an open group

1. Open the main navigation.
2. Tap **Create Group** and select the forum option.
3. Configure the group name and settings.
4. Share the invitation link with participants.

---

## Media support

Both private groups and open groups support:
- **Text messages**
- **Images** — with optional text captions (see [Text with Images](/docs/features/text-with-images))
- **Voice messages** — tap to record and send encrypted audio
- **Location sharing** — share offline map coordinates

---

## Key behaviors

- **Real-time sync** — messages sync automatically when peers are connected.
- **Offline support** — compose messages offline; they are sent when connectivity is restored.
- **Message retention** — configure automatic message deletion per group (see [Message Retention](/docs/features/message-retention)).
- **Notifications** — customize notification settings per group.
