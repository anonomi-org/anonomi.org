---
title: Message Retention
description: Automatically delete old messages on a per-group basis.
---

Message Retention lets you configure **automatic message deletion** for any blog, forum, or private group. Messages older than the configured period are removed from your device.

---

## Overview

In high-threat environments, keeping old messages indefinitely increases exposure if a device is seized or compromised. Message Retention gives you control over how long messages are kept, on a per-group basis.

---

## Where to find it

Message Retention is available in the **overflow menu** of any:
- Blog
- Forum
- Private group

Open the group, tap the overflow menu (three dots), and select **Message Retention**.

---

## Available options

| Option | Behavior |
|--------|----------|
| **7 days** | Messages older than 7 days are automatically deleted |
| **30 days** | Messages older than 30 days are automatically deleted |
| **90 days** | Messages older than 90 days are automatically deleted |
| **180 days** | Messages older than 180 days are automatically deleted |
| **Keep forever** | No automatic deletion (default) |

---

## User flow

1. Open a blog, forum, or private group.
2. Tap the **overflow menu** (three dots).
3. Select **Message Retention**.
4. Choose a retention period.
5. The setting takes effect immediately.

Messages older than the selected period are deleted. New messages will be automatically deleted once they exceed the retention window.

---

## Key behaviors

- **Local-only** — Message Retention deletes messages from your device only. It does not affect other participants' copies.
- **Per-group** — Each group has its own retention setting. Changing one group does not affect others.
- **Retroactive** — When you set a retention period, existing messages older than the period are deleted immediately.
- **Thread coherence** — When a message in a thread is deleted by retention, the thread structure remains intact for remaining messages.
- **Reversible** — You can change the retention period or switch back to "Keep forever" at any time. This stops future automatic deletions but does not restore already-deleted messages.
